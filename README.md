## 🌍 Project Overview
Tutrugbu (ISO 639-3: ttr), also known as Nyagbo, is spoken by ~14,000 people across 8 villages east of Lake Volta, north of Hohoe, Afadzato South District, Ghana. Currently ranked #5529 on Mozilla Common Voice.

We are building an open-source AI suite: speech data collection, ASR, and TTS using Google DeepMind Gemini / Gemma 3.

## 🎯 Goals
- Collect & transcribe 500+ hours of Tutrugbu speech
- Fine-tune ASR and TTS models (Tutrugbu <-> English/Ewe)
- Release open dataset to Mozilla Common Voice
- Deploy offline Android literacy app for rural schools
